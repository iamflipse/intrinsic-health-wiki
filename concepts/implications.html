What It Means For You

The intrinsic–realized framework reframes several everyday health questions:

QuestionAnswer under this framework
Does health decline with age?Intrinsic health certainly tends to decline with age; realized health may not — if contextual factors (environment, resources, meaning) are right.
Does a cold affect your health?If you recover fully, intrinsic health never changed — your immune response did exactly what it should. Your realized health may have suffered in the meantime.
How does trauma affect health?Early-life trauma can become biologically embedded, causing long-term decrements in both intrinsic and realized health; both can be modified with appropriate treatment.

Bigger picture

  • Challenges the dominant biomedical model: health isn't only a list of diseases, and organisms aren't understood by static decomposition into parts.
  • Challenges "a pill for everything": health is a gradient you can build, preserve, and restore across the whole life course — preventing disease and maintaining quality of life at all ages.
