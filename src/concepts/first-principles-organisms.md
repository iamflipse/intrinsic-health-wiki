# First-Principles View of the Organism

Rather than building health up from molecules (reductionism), the post reasons **downward** from what an organism *is*:

- **Complex systems theory:** the body's components (molecules → cells → tissues → organs) interact through **feedback loops**, so changes in one part don't produce simple, predictable effects elsewhere (dieting example: eat less → body stores more fat + hunger response → you may *gain* weight).
- **Evolutionary biology:** these systems were optimized over billions of years for one overriding task: **hold themselves together long enough to survive and reproduce**.

**The tightrope-walker analogy (against the dominant biomedical paradigm):**

- Reductionism would hunt for a drug to "decontract" the left calf muscle of a fallen walker, missing that the contraction *was* the balance-restoring response.
- A walker who **freezes rigid** drifts and falls; one who **swings wildly** also falls. Balance is maintained by **continuous micro-adjustments of many muscles** — dynamic equilibrium.
- The body does this every second, in thousands of dimensions, adjusting to constant internal/external fluctuations.

**Intrinsic health = the body's capacity to maintain that dynamic equilibrium.**
