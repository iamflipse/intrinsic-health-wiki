# What Is Health?

The WHO defines health as "a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity." Aspiration, but hard to measure:

- **How do we measure it?** A luxury-car envy — is that a decrement of well-being, and therefore of health?
- **What are the limits of health?**

Cohen (with Martin Picard and colleagues, *Science Advances* 2025) argues health is not just a vague aspiration but a **property of biological and psychological systems** that can be understood scientifically and **quantified**.

**What a measurable health metric would enable:**

- See if a new diet is actually working for you.
- Test medical interventions by **overall benefit**, not one or two symptoms.
- Follow **population health** objectively and assess which policies help or harm.

The post pushes Martin Picard's earlier "first principles" line further: health understood from first principles, with energy as a central variable.
